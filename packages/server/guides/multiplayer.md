## Over this MCP connection

The client's own guide that the skill names, `packages/net/README.md`, is `zirbel://guide/net-api` here.

Over this connection you make the skill's changes with `write_files` (`zirbel://guide/files`): `zirbel.json`, `zirbel-adapter.js`, and the game's entry module with its one line, in one proposal. Writing `zirbel.json` with a `players.max` above 1 makes the game a net game for that many players, up to 16, as importing the folder would. Section 9 runs a Zirbel host of your own to test with; this game reaches its players once the owner applies your change, publishes it and offers it in the lobby.
